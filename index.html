About Kandhan Groups

We don't just build structures. We build trust.

Kandhan Groups is a full-service construction company: builders, developers and consultants. We handle every stage of a project, including architecture, planning, approvals, execution and project management, with quality you can see and timelines you can count on.

From family homes to large industrial facilities, ponds and water bodies to Sewage Treatment Plants, our engineers and craftsmen bring precision, transparency and care to every square foot.

✓ Licensed Engineers ✓ Transparent Pricing ✓ On-time Delivery ✓ End-to-End Service

What We Do

Complete Construction Solutions, Under One Roof

Whatever you need built, designed or managed, our specialist teams deliver it.

Residential Construction

Independent homes, villas, apartments and gated communities, built with premium materials and finishes.

Commercial & Corporate

Offices, showrooms, complexes and company campuses designed for business and built to impress.

Industrial Construction

Factories, warehouses, PEB and steel sheds with heavy-duty flooring, delivered fast and to specification.

Complete Civil Works

Foundations, RCC structures, roads, compound walls, drainage, retaining walls and all structural work.

Pond & Water Bodies

Farm ponds, fish ponds, temple tanks, lakes, check dams and rainwater harvesting structures.

Architecture & Planning

Concept design, 2D/3D plans, structural design, Vastu-compliant layouts, elevations and approvals.

PMC: Project Management

Project management consultancy covering cost control, scheduling, quality audits, vendor management and site supervision.

Sewage Treatment Plants

STP design, construction, installation and commissioning for apartments, industries and institutions.
